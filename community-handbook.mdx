# Community Handbook

This handbook explains the general expectations and procedures for participating in Iowa State Roleplay (ISRP).

!!! info "Who this applies to"
    These expectations apply to all members using ISRP's Discord and participating in ISRP community activities, unless a rule is clearly limited to a particular area.

## 1. Respect and conduct

- Treat members respectfully, even when disagreements occur.
- Harassment, targeted hostility, discrimination, threats, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable.
- Do not use slurs or hateful language.
- Do not encourage arguments or disrupt conversations.
- Follow reasonable directions from authorized staff.

## 2. Discord and communications

- Use channels for their intended purposes.
- Keep messages, usernames, profile content, and shared media appropriate for the community.
- Do not spam, flood channels, or repeatedly post unwanted content.
- Do not impersonate another member, staff member, or official account.
- Do not share another person's private information.
- Do not advertise or solicit without permission from ISRP management.

## 3. Roleplay and community participation

- Keep roleplay interactions within the applicable game and server rules.
- Do not exploit bugs, intentionally disrupt sessions, or interfere with another member's ability to participate.
- Follow session host instructions and any published session procedures.
- Keep out-of-character disagreements separate from roleplay where practical.
- Do not present fictional roleplay authority as real-world authority.

## 4. Staff and leadership

- Staff members are expected to act professionally and apply published policies consistently.
- Staff must not misuse permissions, threaten members with authority, or use their position for personal disputes.
- Members may respectfully ask for clarification or request a management review.
- Staff decisions should be based on available information, not rumors or personal preference.

## 5. Reports and appeals

Members who have a concern should use the official reporting or support process provided in the ISRP Discord.

When submitting a report, include relevant context and available evidence. Do not edit evidence in a misleading way or encourage others to coordinate false reports.

A member who believes a staff action was incorrect may request a review through the designated appeal process. Appeals should remain respectful and should address the decision and supporting facts.

## 6. Enforcement

Rule concerns may be addressed through a staff reminder, moderation action, removal from an activity, or further review, depending on the circumstances and the approved ISRP punishment policy.

Staff should document formal actions and provide a clear reason when practical. Serious or repeated concerns may be referred to higher management.

## 7. Changes to this handbook

ISRP management may update this handbook. Members are responsible for reviewing announcements about policy changes.
