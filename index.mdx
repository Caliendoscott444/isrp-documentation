## About this documentation

These pages are the central reference for ISRP members and staff. Members are expected to review the rules and follow official staff directions.

**Document status:** Initial ISRP draft. The original Jackson County GitBook page contents were not available to this build, so this is newly written starter material—not a verified word-for-word conversion. ISRP management should review and approve all policies, penalty levels, and training requirements before treating them as final.
