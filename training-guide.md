# Training Guide

This guide sets out a general framework for preparing, conducting, and documenting ISRP training.

!!! info "Management approval required"
    This is an initial ISRP draft, not a verified conversion of the original Jackson County training guide. ISRP management should add the official training stages, required terminology, pass marks, instructor ranks, and retest rules before use.

## 1. Training purpose

Training should help members understand ISRP procedures, communicate clearly, work cooperatively, and meet the expectations of the role or department they are joining.

Training must be conducted respectfully. Instructors should explain expectations, demonstrate procedures, allow questions, and provide useful feedback.

## 2. Training roles

**Trainee**
- Arrives prepared and follows the session instructions.
- Listens to the instructor and asks for clarification when needed.
- Participates respectfully and completes assigned learning activities.

**Instructor**
- Confirms the trainee is eligible for the session.
- Explains the objectives and relevant rules.
- Gives clear instructions and consistent feedback.
- Records the outcome accurately.

**Training supervisor or management**
- Oversees training standards.
- Reviews disputed or unusual results.
- Ensures instructors follow the approved process.
- Updates training material when procedures change.

ISRP management must confirm the official rank requirements for each role.

## 3. Before a training session

Instructors should:

1. Confirm the session time, location, and trainee list.
2. Check that the correct training materials are available.
3. Explain the session objectives and expected conduct.
4. Confirm that trainees understand how to ask questions or request clarification.
5. Make any relevant accessibility or communication arrangements.

## 4. During training

- Explain each topic before evaluating it.
- Demonstrate the expected process in a clear and safe way.
- Give trainees a reasonable opportunity to practice.
- Keep feedback specific, respectful, and relevant.
- Pause to clarify misunderstandings.
- Do not change evaluation standards midway through a session.

## 5. Evaluation

Trainees should be evaluated against criteria published before the session. Depending on the approved ISRP program, evaluation may include:

- Understanding of community and department rules.
- Communication and teamwork.
- Knowledge of role-specific procedures.
- Ability to follow session instructions.
- Professional conduct and sound roleplay judgment.

Do not invent a passing score or mark a trainee as passed unless the approved criteria are met.

## 6. Training outcomes

The final ISRP program should define its official outcomes. A useful record may include:

- **Passed:** The trainee met all required criteria.
- **Needs additional training:** More instruction or practice is required.
- **Not completed:** The session ended before an evaluation could be completed.

Instructors should explain the result and identify any areas that need improvement.

## 7. Training records

Record the following where applicable:

- Trainee's Discord username and user ID.
- Roblox username, if required by the program.
- Instructor and any assisting staff.
- Date and training type.
- Topics or evaluation areas completed.
- Result and relevant feedback.
- Follow-up or retest arrangements.

Keep training records in the designated ISRP staff channels or approved record system. Do not post private trainee information publicly.

## 8. Retests and concerns

A trainee who needs additional training should be told what to review and how to arrange another session under the approved ISRP process.

Concerns about an instructor, evaluation, or training record should be referred to the designated training supervisor or management member.

## 9. Instructor standards

Instructors are expected to:

- Be prepared and familiar with current materials.
- Apply the same published criteria to all trainees.
- Avoid favoritism and personal disputes.
- Keep feedback constructive.
- Maintain accurate records.
- Refer questions outside their authority to management.

## Items ISRP management must finalize

- Official training types and stages.
- Instructor eligibility and minimum rank.
- Required topics and practical evaluations.
- Passing score or pass/fail criteria.
- Retest limits and waiting periods, if any.
- Official training result and logging format.
